Earshot

A clearer voice in the headphones, and the words on screen as they are spoken.

  1. Plug wired headphones into this device.Bluetooth adds a delay that sounds like an echo.
  2. Put this device within arm's reach of the person speaking.A close microphone does more for clarity than any volume setting.
  3. Hand the headphones to the listener, then start.The volume starts low. The listener turns it up.

This page does not record or save anything. Captions use your browser's speech service, which may process audio online. Captions show the words as spoken and do not translate.
